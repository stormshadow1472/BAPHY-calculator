# Physics Experiments — Interactive Laboratory Suite

A production-grade, interactive Engineering & University Physics Laboratory Suite & Data Analysis Web Application.

🌐 **Live Website**: [https://stormshadow1472.github.io/BAPHY-calculator/](https://stormshadow1472.github.io/BAPHY-calculator/)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **12+ Comprehensive Experiment Modules**:
  - **Preparatory Tools**: Screw Gauge (Thickness & Zero Error), Vernier Calipers (Dimensions & Volume), Travelling Microscope (Capillary Bore Radius), Spectrometer (Angle of Prism & Refractive Index).
  - **Semiconductor & Photovoltaic Physics**: Solar Cell Renewable Energy Circuits (I-V, P-V, Fill Factor, Efficiency), Planck's Constant from LED Barrier Potentials, Photodiode Characteristics (Dark vs. Illuminated, Responsivity), Quantum Tunneling through 1D Potential Barriers.
  - **Quantum Physics & Computing**: Electron Diffraction & de Broglie Wavelength, Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle (Single-Slit Diffraction), Single Qubit States & 3D Bloch Sphere Visualizer, Two-Qubit Entangled States, CNOT & SWAP Gates.
- **Dynamic Calculation & Error Analysis Engine**:
  - Instant row-by-row derived variables and statistical metrics.
  - Linear regression ($y = mx + c$), $R^2$ correlation, slope and intercept error margins.
  - Automatic comparison against CODATA scientific standards.
- **100% Canvas Visualizations**:
  - Spectrometer: Interactive $i - \delta$ Minimum Deviation Characteristic Curve.
  - Travelling Microscope: Eyepiece Reticle & Capillary Bore Geometry cross-section with dimension arrows.
  - Single-Slit Diffraction: Fraunhofer Intensity Profile $I(\theta) = I_0 (\sin\beta/\beta)^2$ with overlaid observation minima markers.
  - Quantum Computing: State Probability Distribution Bar Chart and 3D Bloch Sphere.
  - Preparatory Tools: Precision measurement distribution with $\pm\sigma$ standard deviation confidence band.
  - Solar Cell: Dual-series I-V and P-V curves with Maximum Power Point (MPP) crosshairs.
- **Data Import & Report Generation**:
  - Direct copy-paste from Excel, Google Sheets, or CSV files.
  - Export data directly to CSV.
  - Generate formal printable PDF laboratory reports with student name, registration number, and embedded canvas plots.
