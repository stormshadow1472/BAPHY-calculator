# Skills Profile: Physics Computational & Visualizer System

## Technical & Software Engineering
* **Frontend Frameworks & Architecture:** React.js, TypeScript, HTML5/CSS3 — building single-page reactive user interfaces with component-driven design.
* **Data Visualization & Interactive Graphics:** Plotly.js, Chart.js, Recharts — rendering responsive scatter plots, dynamic best-fit lines, residuals, and customizable axis scales.
* **State Management & Client-Side Storage:** React Hooks (useContext, useReducer), `localStorage` / `sessionStorage` — zero-latency reactive updates and offline data persistence.
* **Data Engineering & Document Export:** Client-side CSV parsing/generation, jsPDF, html2canvas — automated laboratory report generation and structured dataset exports.
* **Application Security & Data Isolation:** Client-side execution models, zero-trust data patterns, local-only processing, and strict API/credential isolation.

---

## Mathematics & Scientific Computing
* **Numerical Methods & Regression Analysis:** Implementation of linear regression ($y = mx + c$), non-linear curve fitting, matrix operations, and polynomial interpolation.
* **Statistical Error Quantification:** Computation of Coefficient of Determination ($R^2$), standard deviation, propagation of uncertainty, and percentage error analysis.
* **Dimensional Analysis & Unit Normalization:** Automated unit conversion pipelines mapping diverse laboratory units (nm, eV, Å, kΩ) to standard SI units.

---

## Physics & Engineering Domain Knowledge
* **Quantum & Modern Physics:** Data processing for Planck’s Constant, Fowler-Nordheim quantum tunnelling, de Broglie wavelength electron diffraction, and $e/m$ specific charge ratios.
* **Semiconductor & Solid State Electronics:** Characterization of I-V curves, Hall Effect coefficients, semiconductor band gap energy ($E_g$), and solar cell fill factor/efficiency.
* **Optics, Electromagnetism & Mechanics:** Analysis of diffraction grating wavelengths, Newton's rings, prism refractive index, wire resistivity, and harmonic oscillator dynamics.
